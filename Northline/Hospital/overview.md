# NET.runner-92 hospital overview

[Open the complete furnished hospital](Phase_05/NET-runner-92-hospital-phase-05.blend) · [Final verification and handoff](Phase_05/modeling_progress.md)

[Open the Phase 1 Blender shell](NET-runner-92-hospital.blend)

The hospital is a four-storey medical campus building for first-person stealth and hacking. The ground floor has a covered public entrance to the south and a separate covered ambulance intake on the north. The upper floors transition from inpatient care to surgery and intensive care, then to restricted research. A setback over the central south façade reduces the research floor footprint. The setback is roof area outside the playable shell.

The main block is approximately **72 × 30 m** on the wall grid, with additional ground-floor entrance wings. Finished floor reference elevations are **0.00, 4.20, 8.40 and 12.60 m**. The main roof deck is at **16.80 m**. An 8 m longitudinal column grid, deep perimeter and clinical bays, and stacked service cores give the shell a consistent structural rhythm. Glazing follows the rooms behind it. Wards have broad patient windows, while surgery, service spaces and restricted areas use higher sills where appropriate.

A central east–west concourse handles bed and staff movement. Ward and acute-care halls branch from it, and a central lift lobby connects the twin bed-lift shafts. Two enclosed stairs anchor opposite ends of the building. The intended mission progression takes the west stair to Level 2, the east stair to Level 3 and the west stair to Level 4. The final lab sits near the east end behind a concourse checkpoint and an anteroom. Both stairs serve all four floors. The mission route relies on future access control to make the player alternate stairs.
