## Door and stair layout

- Each end has a continuous enclosed switchback stair with a ground-level exterior egress door. The upper concourse doors connect to full-floor landings. Each storey has two flights of 12 risers at 175 mm, with 290 mm treads and a 2.05 m-deep half-landing. The nominal flight width is 1.97 m before handrails. Upper slabs have modeled stair voids and edge guards. The Level 4 landings terminate the stairs.
- The public vestibule, ambulance vestibule and both grade-level stair exits open onto flush structural aprons. The ambulance route leads into resuscitation, while the public vestibule opens into reception.
- Most clinical rooms use paired sliding leaves. Fire doors and smaller support rooms use hinged leaves with hinge pivots. The Level 4 checkpoint uses stacked sliding leaves so the open panels fit within the concourse width. The two lift shafts have closed landing doors on every floor.
- The main concourse stays clear through the shell, with ward and acute-care halls reached through separate branches. The riser-access room behind the lift core is reached from an adjacent east-side support room on each floor. It is separate from the lift shafts.

## Progression

The intended sequence is **south entrance → Level 1 west stair → Level 2 east stair → Level 3 west stair → Level 4 checkpoint → final lab**. It crosses the ward and surgery floors in opposite directions and brings the player past both departments on each of those floors. Side rooms are available for optional exploration. Both stairs and the checkpoint are passable in the shell. Later mission locks can control entry to the next ascending flight while keeping the hospital's egress paths credible.
