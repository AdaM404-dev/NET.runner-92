# NET.runner-92 intro sound design

The exterior starts with wind, rain and separate pavement droplets spread across the stereo field. The thunder rolls after the electrical flash. Rain loses level as the camera enters the vestibule. Inside, ventilation and electrical hum sit behind short, position-specific electronics and mechanical cues. Early reflections and a short stereo decay place those cues in the hospital. The title gets the strongest bass impact and a developed passage of Scott Buckley's score. The ending uses a distant motor and three monitor tones before a hard cut.

## Mix and editing

`Intro_Master.wav`, `Intro_Music.wav` and `Intro_Effects.wav` are 60.000 s stereo, 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM. Use the master alone or place the two stems at frame 1 at unity gain. The music starts under the entrance, gives space to the clinical and door cues, then crossfades into a later passage whose swell lands at 46.00 s. The master measures −2.00 dBTP using fourfold oversampling. The final 1.50 s is digital silence.
