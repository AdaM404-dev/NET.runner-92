# Quarantine storytelling

The player enters after the evacuation. The two outer gates have been shut in place, with parked crash blocks and reinforcement around their drives. A broken public badge terminal remains near the staff booth. From there the most direct route leads across a marked crossing and under a secondary contamination scanner. The working side indicator suggests the scanner still has a local power circuit.

The original entry canopy and sliding doors are visible from the scanner. The freestanding Northline monument reads **FACILITY STATUS: QUARANTINED**, with public access suspended and systems active. One identity bar is dark. The local information pillar has lost its link. A visitor satchel and case sit by damaged benches.

The southwest court held people before evacuation. **MEDICAL SCREENING**, **DECONTAMINATION** and **EVACUATION ASSEMBLY** identify the stages by function. Closed reserve trunks and abandoned carts remain within reach of the shelter aisles. A downed temporary screen, shifted ballast and leaves at its base indicate damage after the structures had been set up.

At the north drive, the intact E-12 and damaged E-09 show where the emergency vehicles stopped. Their chargers, staging lines, supply cabinet and empty trolley imply a workflow from the rear stretcher doors to the hospital. The transport path is clear today.

The northeast gate reads **BIOLOGICAL CONTAINMENT AREA** and **AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**. **ACCESS REVOKED** is posted by the reader. A powered mast and dock contrast with dark perimeter sensors and dead fixtures. These signs show where authorities contained movement and what still has power. The reason for the lockdown remains undisclosed.
