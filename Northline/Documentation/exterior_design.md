# Northline quarantine grounds

[Open the Phase 4 Blender project](../Environment/Hospital_Quarantine_Grounds.blend) · [Validation and renders](exterior_validation.md)

## Site layout

The site uses the hospital's existing metric origin and orientation. +X is east and +Y is north. The perimeter is 104 × 78 m, from X −52 to +52 and Y −40 to +38. The original building is about 72 × 30 m, with its public and ambulance wings projecting to the south and north. All four furnished levels are in the same Blender file.

| Area | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| South public approach | The sealed outer gate marks the former vehicle entrance. The player starts inside the enclosure. A marked crossing, raised vehicle stops and contamination portal lead to the public plaza and its original sliding doors. |
| Southwest screening court | Two portable shelters served screening and decontamination, with a nearby evacuation assembly point and transport carts. The player can enter the shelter aisles or follow the west service road. |
| Southeast visitor court | Eight marked visitor spaces, a charger and a planted shoulder occupy the east side of the plaza. |
| Side roads | A service lane follows each short end of the hospital. Both original stair egress landings meet those roads through short paved links. |
| North emergency side | The north loop reaches the original ambulance canopy, vestibule and resuscitation doors. Two parked ambulances and charging posts sit on the far side of the drive. |
| Northeast maintenance cage | A closed internal gate secures emergency stock, the drone dock and service controls. A signed approach leads to the reader beside the gate. |
| Boundary | Welded anti-climb fence and precast screening close the property. Both vehicle gates are physically shut. A narrow outer verge, low embankment and tree silhouettes give depth to the views. |

The public entrance is at (0, −19.20 m) and ambulance intake is at (−22, 19.00 m). The east and west egress doors are at X ±36 m and Y −3.68 m. The new approach surfaces sit within 33 mm of the original landing elevations on the sampled routes. Props stay outside the hospital envelope and canopy footprint. The original building, windows, sliding leaves, doors and furnishing remain in place.

## Materials and atmosphere

Water-darkened asphalt, ground concrete and coated metal use Blender Principled shaders with position-based color, roughness and bump. Shallow water-film meshes follow low points around drainage channels. Weathering concentrates on settling seams, exposed hardware, shelter footings and runoff. Sedges grow through cracks and behind the curb. Scrub and small trees occupy soil strips, and ivy stays on the northern boundary wall.

The default review is overcast evening. Cold illumination keeps circulation legible, while working canopy and street fixtures pick out the routes. Red indicates a locked control point, amber indicates quarantine, and muted mint identifies powered equipment. The monument's status line has an editable intermittent flicker. Low ground haze and an offsite silhouette belt keep attention on the hospital grounds.
