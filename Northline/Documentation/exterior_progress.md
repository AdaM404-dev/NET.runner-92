# Exterior development progress

| Stage | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A · Terrain and boundaries | Built the compact 104 × 78 m site, gravel shoulders, welded fence, precast walls, sealed gates and perimeter collision proxies. |
| B · Roads and access | Connected the main plaza, side service lanes, ambulance loop, visitor bays and original landings. Nine preliminary grade probes passed. |
| C · Quarantine infrastructure | Added the public scanner, closed maintenance checkpoint, staff booth, medical screening and decontamination shelters, evacuation assembly point, signage and crash barriers. |
| D · Futuristic environment | Modeled two ground ambulances, charging and biometric terminals, PTZ units, disabled sensors, drone dock, cleaning crawler and selectively functioning fixtures. |
| E · Abandonment and realism | Added localized surface damage, litter, moss, runoff water, volunteer vegetation and the sparse offsite silhouette belt. |
| F · Lighting and presentation | Saved eight review cameras, the overcast lighting and wet-weather volume. Reviewed the draft compositions, adjusted framing and rendered eight final Cycles views. |
| G · Validation | Matched the original hospital data, sampled eight routes, tested the continuous perimeter proxies and completed an 11-mesh FBX smoke test. Corrected headroom, a mast location and sign placement. |

[Blender deliverable](../Environment/Hospital_Quarantine_Grounds.blend) · [Final validation and render links](exterior_validation.md)

The delivered model includes all four original hospital floors, with the new exterior assets in their own collection. The earlier cinematic remains a separate project. Gameplay logic, material conversion and runtime checks are the next integration stage.
