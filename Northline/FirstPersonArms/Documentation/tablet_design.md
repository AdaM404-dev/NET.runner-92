# LATCH-07 forearm interface

The device is a purpose-built service interface for a 2087 infiltration operative. The asymmetric titanium chassis follows the left forearm along its long axis. Two curved liners and independent webbing bands distribute load around the sleeve. Each band has a pair of external adjustment and locking fittings. Protective guards sit outside the display travel. A recessed Torx pattern, radio aperture, cooling slots, service release and cyan/amber indicators identify the distinct service areas.

| Feature | Nominal dimension |
| --- | --- |
| Chassis footprint | 199 × 103 mm |
| Active display | 150 × 76 mm |
| Display deployment | 4.6 mm normal to the display |
| Webbing band width | 18 mm each |
| Working orientation | Outer left forearm, display toward the camera during hacking |

`Device_Mount` follows the left lower arm. `Device_Display` carries the thin screen, ceramic carrier and its backplate. The lift is a short linear movement within the guard perimeter. During cancel and unequip the panel returns flush within the guard profile as the arm leaves view.

`NR92_Display_Surface` is a dedicated four-vertex plane with material `NR92_Display_Surface`. UV0 covers the complete 0–1 square. The upper edge is V=1 and the edge toward the wrist is U=1. The Blender boot labels and signal bars are preview geometry in their own collection. Permanent housing marks such as “LATCH // 07” are part of the device.

The authored tap lands in the lower action region. Three prototype target coordinates cover the left, middle and right portion of that region.
